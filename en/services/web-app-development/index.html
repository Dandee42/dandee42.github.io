Custom Web App Development

When spreadsheets and off-the-shelf tools stop being enough, it's time for a custom app.

Apps that adapt to you, not the other way around

Companies often track orders in Excel, email spreadsheets back and forth and retype data between systems. Off-the-shelf software, on the other hand, forces them to adapt their processes to its logic. A custom web app does exactly what you need and nothing more.

What I can build for you

  • Internal systems and records: instead of shared spreadsheets, a clear app for orders, clients or stock, with user permissions and change history.
  • Client portals: a place where your customers find their orders, documents or the status of their project.
  • Dashboards and reports: data from multiple sources in one place, without manual exports.
  • MVPs and prototypes: a first version of your product to test the idea on the market.
  • Extensions to existing systems: adding features where off-the-shelf software stops.

What's the difference between a website and a web app?

A website presents you, and visitors read it. A web app is for getting work done: users log in, enter and edit data, and the app works with it, for example by calculating, sorting or sending notifications. It runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install and it works on desktops and phones.

Connected to the tools you already use

An app doesn't have to stand alone. With my experience in automation (n8n) and APIs, I'll connect it to the tools you already use, such as invoicing, your CRM, email or spreadsheets, so data flows between them automatically. Where it makes sense, I'll add AI too: sorting documents, extracting data or searching company documents with RAG.

Fast AI-assisted development, reliable thanks to tests

I build apps in Django (Python) or Next.js, depending on what suits your project better, and use AI tools such as Claude Code and Cursor during development. The routine parts of the code get written faster, so you get a working version sooner and for less. Speed must not come at the expense of quality, so I cover key features with automated tests (Playwright) that check the app still works after every change.

How we'll work together

  1. Free initial consultation: we'll discuss what problem the app should solve and who will use it.
  2. Paid prototype for CZK 30,000: you'll see how the app will work before you commit to the whole project. If we continue, the full amount counts towards the price of the app.
  3. Development in stages: I build the app step by step and fine-tune it with you along the way.
  4. Testing and deployment: automated tests, going live and handover.
  5. Support and further development: the first month of support is included, then a monthly care plan or an hourly rate.

How much does a custom web app cost?

Prototype for CZK 30,000, web apps from CZK 80,000. The price depends on the number of features, user roles and integrations with other systems. After the prototype you'll know exactly what you'll get and for how much. I'm not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added.

Frequently asked questions

How long does development take?

It depends on the scope. Thanks to AI-assisted development, the first working version is usually ready sooner than with traditional development. You'll get a specific timeline after the prototype.

Do I need a detailed specification?

No. It's enough to describe the problem you're solving. We'll refine the requirements together during the consultation and the prototype.

Can you connect the app to our systems?

If your systems have an API or allow data export, usually yes. We'll check the integration options during the prototype.

Who owns the source code?

You do. When the project is finished, I'll hand over the source code and all access, so anyone can develop the app further.

What do hosting and maintenance cost?

Running the app includes hosting and maintenance. You can have it looked after through a monthly care plan, or pay only for the changes you order.
