Custom Website Development

A website that works for you, even when you don't.

A website that's more than a business card

Most small business websites look nice, but that's where their job ends. Enquiries get lost in an inbox, nobody knows where visitors came from, and after a year the site is slow because of dozens of plugins. I build custom business websites that are fast, easy to find in search and, above all, connected to the way you work.

I came to IT from online marketing and e-commerce, so I don't see a website as just code, but as a tool that should bring you customers.

Who a custom website is for

  • Freelancers and consultants who need a trustworthy presentation and a steady flow of enquiries.
  • Small businesses and service providers who want their website connected to a CRM, calendar or spreadsheets.
  • Companies with international clients who need a website in Czech and English.
  • Anyone who has outgrown a template website because it's slow, hard to edit or nobody finds it.

What you get

  • Structure and content built around your customers: a website shaped by what visitors are looking for and what they should do next.
  • SEO foundations: heading structure, metadata, sitemap, structured data and clean URLs, so search engines understand your site.
  • Works on every device: phone, tablet and desktop.
  • Multiple languages: a website in Czech and English, set up correctly for search engines.
  • Traffic analytics: Google Analytics 4 with a cookie consent banner.
  • Forms connected to your processes: see the next section.

Enquiries that handle themselves

This is where my websites differ from the usual ones. Because I specialise in process automation (n8n), your website can work with enquiries straight away:

  • saving each enquiry to your CRM, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet,
  • sending the customer an automatic confirmation,
  • notifying you instantly by email or chat,
  • sorting enquiries with AI by type or urgency,
  • reminding you when an enquiry hasn't been answered.

Find out more about connecting systems under Intelligent Automation.

A tested website that keeps working

A form that silently stops sending costs you business, and nobody notices. I also do automated testing (Playwright), so key parts of your website, such as forms and bookings, can be covered by automated tests that check they still work after every change.

Why a custom static website

I don't build websites on WordPress or on website-builder templates. I build fast, custom static websites:

  • Speed: pages are prepared in advance, so they load instantly.
  • Security: there's no database and no plugins that need constant updates and are the most common target of attacks.
  • Low running costs: hosting a static website is much cheaper.
  • Content editing: I'll add a simple editor where you can change text and images yourself. I'll handle bigger changes through a care plan or at an hourly rate.

How we'll work together

  1. Free initial consultation: we'll talk about your services, your customers and what the website should achieve.
  2. Structure and content plan: I'll prepare the page structure and recommendations for the copy.
  3. Development: you'll see the work in progress and can comment along the way.
  4. Integrations and testing: forms, analytics and automations.
  5. Launch and support: I'll launch the website on your domain, and fixes and small edits are included for the first month.

How much does a custom website cost?

Business websites from CZK 24,900. The final price depends on the number of pages, language versions and integrations with other systems. After the consultation you'll get a quote. I'm not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added.

After launch you can choose a monthly care plan (hosting, maintenance, backups and small edits) or pay only for individual changes at an hourly rate.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the scope and on how quickly the copy and materials are ready. You'll get a specific timeline in the quote after the initial consultation.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

Yes. I'll add a simple editor where you can change text and images yourself, no coding needed. I'll handle bigger changes, such as new sections or features, through a care plan or at an hourly rate.

Who will own the website?

You will. The domain and the content are yours, and I'll hand over all access when the project is finished.

What will the running costs be?

A static website has low hosting costs. You can have the website looked after through a monthly care plan, or pay only for the changes you order.

What will you need from me?

Information about your services, your logo, photos and material for the copy. I'll help you with the content structure.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. I'll review your current website and build the new one so that it keeps its search rankings, for example by redirecting old URLs.

What's the difference between a website and a landing page?

A website presents you as a whole and has multiple pages. A landing page is a single page with a single goal, usually for an ad campaign.

Do you build online shops?

No. I focus on business websites, landing pages and custom web apps.
