Automated Data Reporting

A report that writes itself — delivered to your inbox every week without a single click.

Reports that don't wait for you — you don't wait for them

Manual report creation is one of the most common time-wasters in companies. Every week or month, someone downloads data, copies it to Excel, formats it, and sends it. This entire process can be fully automated — from data collection through calculations to sending the finished report.

What reporting automation includes:

  • Data collection: Google Analytics, Search Console, CRM, databases, spreadsheets.
  • Calculations and aggregation: KPIs, trends, period-over-period comparisons.
  • Output formatting: PDF report, Excel, email, or Slack message.
  • Scheduled delivery: Daily, weekly, or monthly — always at the right time to the right people.
  • AI commentary: Optionally add an AI summary of key changes and recommendations.
