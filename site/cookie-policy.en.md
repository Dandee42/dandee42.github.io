## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences or to track website traffic.

## What cookies does this website use?

This website uses only analytical cookies via **Google Analytics 4** (GA4), operated by Google LLC.

| Cookie | Purpose | Expiry |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `_ga` | Distinguishes individual visitors | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Stores session state | 2 years |

No marketing or functional third-party cookies are used on this website.

## How is your consent obtained?

On your first visit, a notice appears where you can accept or decline analytical cookies. Without your consent, no cookies are set — GA4 runs in anonymous mode without storing any data in your browser.

## How to withdraw consent?

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the button below — the consent notice will reappear and you can change your choice.
