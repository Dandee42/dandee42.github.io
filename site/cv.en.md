# About me

At the beginning of 2024, I decided to make a major career change and began working intensively on transitioning from the role of online marketer to the world of IT. As part of this transformation, I completed several training courses focused on **web application development and testing**, particularly using Python.

Gradually, I learned not only how to test manually, but also how to automate tests using **Playwright** and Selenium, create simple applications in the **Django** platform, and later discover the potential of process automation using **n8n**.

## Why IT and building products

In one of my positions as an online marketer, I was fortunate enough to be able to look over the shoulders of programmers developing an e-shop, which opened up a whole new world for me. I enjoyed participating in the development of the e-shop step by step, coming up with new functionalities, testing, and proposing suitable solutions to current problems.

This experience inspired me to switch to IT and laid the foundation for my current career path – **AI Product Builder**.

## Where I am currently headed and what I can offer you

My experience in **e-commerce** and online marketing helps me look at every project through a business lens: what the product should achieve and for whom. Today I build **web applications** in Django and Next.js, **AI solutions** on top of company data and **automations** in n8n, from the first idea through a prototype to launch. I use AI tools in development to get to a working product faster, and **Playwright** tests make sure nothing breaks along the way. I keep security, scalability and maintainability in mind, so the product holds up as it grows.

My goal is to build products that not only save time, but also make sense.

## What do I know?

I have experience in developing and testing web applications. I also enjoy devising process automation, whether for improving business processes or in my personal life.
